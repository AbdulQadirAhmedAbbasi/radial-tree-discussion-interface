WELCOME

Evaluating the Radial Tree Discussion Interface

In this reader study, you will evaluate the Radial Tree Discussion Interface: a visual tool for navigating public responses to reported scientific research. We are testing the interface and its features—not your knowledge of science or the quality of Reddit comments. The discussions are examples used to try the interface. Describe what the interface helped you do, rather than whether you agree with commenters.

What to expect: a short guided practice, a brief explanation of the study, two discussion-exploration trials, and a closing questionnaire. Allow approximately 25–35 minutes. Please complete this in one sitting: refreshing will restart the session. Use a laptop or desktop browser for best results.

Participation & data handling

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time before submission by closing this page. The study compiles task responses, ratings, elapsed times, selected comment IDs, and interface interactions into a downloadable file. It does not request your real identity or ask you to judge scientific correctness. Responses remain inside this browser until you download a JSON file. This website does not transmit your answers to a server. You must send the downloaded file to the researcher through the agreed channel. Please read the researcher-provided participant information and data-protection notice before participating. Do not include identifying information in your answers.

STUDY INTRODUCTION

Evaluating the Radial Tree Discussion Interface

Science communicators often need to inspect lengthy public discussions without reading every comment. You will explore the same type of radial-tree interface in two display options and tell us how its visual features support overview, navigation, and tracing replies. This is an interface evaluation, not a test of Reddit content or scientific understanding.

  • How the radial tree works: the center represents the original Reddit post; branches and depth rings show who replied to whom. The right reading panel shows original text and conversation context.
  • Basic radial tree: every comment circle is the same size. You can select comments, inspect replies, search, and zoom.
  • Radial tree with visual cues: additional colors mark literal question marks (amber), URLs (teal), and Markdown quotations (pink); split colors indicate multiple cues. Node size also varies with the amount of comment text. These indicators help locate information, not assess importance or accuracy.
  • In either option: gray outlined nodes indicate comments whose text was deleted, removed, or not captured; their reply connections remain important. Search highlights matching nodes in the tree; it does not filter away the right-hand conversation.

You will try both display options on different discussions. Their order varies by participant. In your answers, describe your experience using the Radial Tree Discussion Interface, not whether individual Reddit comments or scientific claims were good or bad.

Focus on ease of overview, finding comments, following replies, and useful or distracting visual cues. You may use search and zoom. Search highlights matching nodes in the radial tree; selecting a result brings its conversation into the right reading panel.
